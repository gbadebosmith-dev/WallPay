Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


How do I earn crypto rewards on WallPay?

Open the WallPay app, browse the available short video adverts, and watch them through to completion. Once a video finishes playing, your crypto micro-reward is automatically credited to your in-app WallPay balance.

Why didn't I receive a reward after watching an advert?

To ensure rewards are credited properly, please make sure:
1. You watch the entire advert until the close button appears.
2. You have a stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data) when the advert finishes.
3. You are not using a VPN, ad-blocker, or private DNS service that may interfere with advert verification.
If a reward still does not appear after restarting the app, please contact us with the approximate time you watched the advert.

How do I withdraw my earnings to my crypto wallet?

1. Go to the Wallet / Withdraw tab inside the WallPay app.
2. Ensure your balance meets the minimum withdrawal threshold.
3. Enter your external wallet address carefully for speed app.
4. Confirm your transfer.
Please double-check your wallet address before confirming. Cryptocurrency transactions are irreversible once broadcast to the network.

Is WallPay free to use?

Yes. WallPay is completely free to download and use. There are no subscriptions or upfront fees required to watch adverts and earn rewards.
